Privacy Policy — Terastream

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Terastream ("we", "our") is a Messenger chatbot and web tool that helps users retrieve file listings and streaming links from public Terabox share links. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Messages you send to our Facebook Page via Messenger — message text (e.g. Terabox share links you submit) and your Page-scoped sender ID, used solely to generate a reply.
  • Comments on our Facebook Page posts — comment text and author ID, used solely to reply to your comment.
  • Page engagement metadata — aggregated, non-identifying information about interactions with our Page (e.g. that a comment or message event occurred), used for basic diagnostics.

We do not collect your Facebook password, email address, friends list, or any data beyond what is needed to operate the chatbot.

2. How We Use Data

  • To reply to your messages and comments (e.g. returning the file list for a Terabox link you shared).
  • To diagnose technical issues (we log only event metadata such as timestamps and event types — never message contents or access tokens).

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Retention

Message and comment contents are processed in real time to generate a reply and are not stored permanently on our servers. Diagnostic metadata (timestamps, event counts) is kept for a maximum of 30 days and then discarded.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of any data associated with your interactions at any time by messaging our Facebook Page ("Terastream") with the word DELETE, or by contacting us (see below). We will confirm deletion within 7 days.

5. Children

Terastream is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, message the Terastream Facebook Page.
